# TOEIC Listening (Parts 1 to 4)

Two sections, kept separate and cross-linked. **Strategy** is how students work through the test. **Skills** is the English that makes the strategy work.

## Strategy: the two pillars
| Pillar | Part 1 | Part 2 | Part 3 | Part 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Locate | Look at the picture before the audio | Listen for the question word | Read the questions in the preview time | Read the questions in the preview time |
| Answer | Choose the sentence that matches what you see | Choose the reply that fits the question | Listen for the signal, then choose | Listen for the signal, then choose |

Students use the preview time before each set to read the questions and options. That is the main habit this section builds.

## Skills: the English behind the strategy
| Part | Skill areas | Linked types |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Picture vocabulary; present continuous and passive descriptions | TQ-1-1 to TQ-1-3 |
| 2 | Question forms; sound-alike words; indirect replies | TQ-2-1 to TQ-2-3 |
| 3 | Everyday workplace speech; reductions; accents | TQ-3-1 to TQ-3-3 |
| 4 | Announcement, voicemail and talk patterns; numbers, times and dates | TQ-4-1 to TQ-4-3 |

## Dependency
Audio production method must be decided before Phase 6 (open question 2). Reading is built first for that reason.
