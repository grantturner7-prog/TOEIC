# TOEIC Listening (Parts 1 to 4)

Two sections, kept separate and cross-linked. **Strategy** is how students work through the test. **Skills** is the English that makes the strategy work.

## Strategy
The pillars adapt to each part. Parts 3 and 4 use **Locate** (find what to listen for) and **Answer**. Parts 1 and 2 are closer to **Catch** (get the key words) and **Answer** (match, then rule out).

| Part | Locate / Catch | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Look at the picture first and predict the vocabulary (people, objects, positions) | Choose the sentence that matches what you see; rule out sound-alikes |
| 2 | Catch the first two or three words; nothing is printed | Match the answer type; rule out options that echo a word but do not answer |
| 3 | Read the questions and options during the directions and the gaps; look at any graphic first | Listen for the signal, then choose |
| 4 | Same as Part 3 | Same as Part 3 |

Habits this section builds (indicative; check exact direction and gap lengths against the ETS sample test and examinee handbook before publishing numbers):
- There is no separate preview pause. The recording plays continuously. Students read ahead during the spoken directions and in the short gaps between questions.
- In Parts 3 and 4, students read the next set while still answering the current one.
- Students answer every question; there is no penalty for a wrong answer.

## Skills: the English behind the strategy
| Part | Skill areas | Linked types |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Picture vocabulary and positions; present continuous and passive descriptions; sound-alike words | TQ-1-1 to TQ-1-3 |
| 2 | Question forms; indirect replies; repeated-word and similar-sound distractors | TQ-2-1 to TQ-2-3 |
| 3 | Everyday workplace speech; connected speech and reductions; speaker-intent phrases | TQ-3-1 to TQ-3-3 |
| 4 | Announcement, voicemail and talk patterns; numbers, times and dates; paraphrase between audio and answer | TQ-4-1 to TQ-4-3 |

### Skills for every Listening part
| Group | Skill areas |
|---|---|
| Hearing the test | Accents (US, UK, Australian, Canadian); connected speech and reduced forms; numbers, dates and prices |
| Chinese-speaker challenges (explained with zh-TW notes) | Plurals and past-tense endings; word stress; word class |
| Vocabulary and stamina | Workplace vocabulary and review loop; paraphrase; concentration over 45 minutes |

## Dependency
The audio production method (open question 2) must be decided before Phase 5, because the diagnostic extension and Part 3 and 4 credibility depend on it. Reading is still built first.
