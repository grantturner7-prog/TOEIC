# TOEIC Listening & Reading: question types

Source of the part structure: ETS public score guide and test description (Listening 6/25/39/30, Reading 30/16/54, 200 questions in total). The question types below are **our own teaching taxonomy**, written for original content. Each part has at most three types so every breakdown stays short.

Recording-queue slot IDs (`TQ-<part>-<n>`) are where the owner's strategy conversations get filed. They remain the source of truth for strategy content.

## Listening (100 questions, 45 minutes)

### Part 1: Photographs (6 questions)
| Slot | Type | Students learn to |
|---|---|---|
| TQ-1-1 | People and actions | Match the action and who is doing it |
| TQ-1-2 | Objects and scenes | Match what is where in the picture |
| TQ-1-3 | Sound-alike choices | Rule out options that sound similar but describe something else |

### Part 2: Question–response (25 questions)
| Slot | Type | Students learn to |
|---|---|---|
| TQ-2-1 | Wh- questions (who, what, when, where, why, how) | Catch the question word and match the answer type |
| TQ-2-2 | Yes/no, choice and tag questions | Pick the reply that fits, direct or indirect |
| TQ-2-3 | Statements, requests and suggestions | Choose the natural workplace response |

### Part 3: Conversations (39 questions, 13 conversations)
| Slot | Type | Students learn to |
|---|---|---|
| TQ-3-1 | Topic and purpose | Preview the question and hear the main point |
| TQ-3-2 | Detail | Listen for the signal that points to the answer |
| TQ-3-3 | Next step, implied meaning and graphics | Combine the audio with a chart, list or map |

### Part 4: Talks (30 questions, 10 talks)
| Slot | Type | Students learn to |
|---|---|---|
| TQ-4-1 | Topic and purpose | Know who is speaking, to whom, and why |
| TQ-4-2 | Detail | Follow announcements, messages and instructions |
| TQ-4-3 | Next step, implied meaning and graphics | Link the talk to a schedule, chart or list |

## Reading (100 questions, 75 minutes)

### Part 5: Incomplete sentences (30 questions)
| Slot | Type | Students learn to |
|---|---|---|
| TQ-5-1 | Word form and verb form | Read the blank's position and choose the form |
| TQ-5-2 | Function words | Choose the right preposition, conjunction or pronoun |
| TQ-5-3 | Vocabulary and collocation | Choose the word that fits the workplace meaning |

### Part 6: Text completion (16 questions)
| Slot | Type | Students learn to |
|---|---|---|
| TQ-6-1 | Grammar in context | Use the whole text, not one sentence, to pick the form |
| TQ-6-2 | Vocabulary and linking words | Choose the word or connector that fits the message |
| TQ-6-3 | Sentence insertion | Place the sentence that fits the flow of the text |

### Part 7: Reading comprehension (54 questions: 29 single-passage, 25 multiple-passage)
| Slot | Type | Students learn to |
|---|---|---|
| TQ-7-1 | Detail and not-stated | Locate the line that holds the answer |
| TQ-7-2 | Purpose and inference | Decide why the text was written and what it implies |
| TQ-7-3 | Vocabulary, sentence insertion and cross-passage | Use context and link information across texts |

## Cross-check
Types per part: 3 each, 21 slots in total. Question totals: 6 + 25 + 39 + 30 + 30 + 16 + 54 = 200.
