# Listening Parts 1 to 4: strategy cards and Skills

Drafted 2026-10-15 from the curriculum map. **Awaiting the owner's recorded strategy conversations (slots TQ-1-1 to TQ-4-3), which replace these drafts.** The app serves the cards from `content/toeic/strategies/p1.json` to `p4.json` in the IELTS repo. One habit runs through all four parts: there is no separate preview pause, so students read ahead during the spoken directions and the gaps between questions.

## Part 1: Photographs (6 questions)
**Catch** 1. Look at the picture during the direction and name what you see: people, actions, objects, positions. 2. Predict the words you may hear (a verb for each person, a position word for each object). 3. Decide whether the picture is about people, things, or both.
**Answer** 1. Judge each statement as you hear it: is it true of the picture, yes or no? 2. Keep the one that is fully true; a statement that is half true is wrong. 3. Rule out sound-alikes and words that belong to the picture but not to the sentence.
Skills: picture vocabulary and positions; present continuous and passive descriptions; sound-alike words.

## Part 2: Question-response (25 questions)
**Catch** 1. Nothing is printed, so listen for the first two or three words: the question word, or the fact that it is a statement. 2. Decide what kind of answer fits: a time, a place, a person, yes or no, a reaction. 3. Hold the question in mind while the three responses play.
**Answer** 1. Match the answer type; an indirect reply ("I'll check the calendar") can still be the answer. 2. Rule out responses that echo a word from the question or answer a different question word. 3. Choose and move on; the next question starts at once.
Skills: question forms; indirect replies; repeated-word and similar-sound distractors.

## Part 3: Conversations (39 questions)
**Locate** 1. Read the three questions and their options during the directions and the pauses; underline what each asks for. 2. Look at any graphic first and decide what the audio must tell you to use it. 3. Listen for the signal that points to each answer, in the order of the questions.
**Answer** 1. Choose as soon as you hear the signal; the questions follow the conversation's order. 2. For intent questions, use the lines just before and after the quoted words. 3. Read the next set's questions while the eight-second pauses run.
Skills: everyday workplace speech; connected speech and reductions; speaker-intent phrases.

## Part 4: Talks (30 questions)
**Locate** 1. Read the questions first and decide who is likely speaking and why. 2. Look at any graphic first. 3. Listen for the signals in order: an announcement, a voicemail or a talk follows a predictable shape.
**Answer** 1. Topic and purpose usually come in the first two sentences; details and the next step come later. 2. Match paraphrases: the option rarely repeats the audio word for word. 3. Read ahead during the pauses; every question gets an answer.
Skills: announcement, voicemail and talk patterns; numbers, times and dates; paraphrase between audio and answer.
