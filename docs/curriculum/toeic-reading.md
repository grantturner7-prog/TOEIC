# TOEIC Reading (Parts 5 to 7)

Two sections, kept separate and cross-linked. **Strategy** is how students work through the test. **Skills** is the English that makes the strategy work.

## Strategy
The two pillars are **Locate** (find the clue) and **Answer** (choose and check). They adapt to each part; Part 5 is closer to a quick routine than a search.

| Part | Locate | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Spot the clue: the word class the blank needs, from the words around it | Choose by form first, then meaning; move on quickly |
| 6 | Read the sentences before and after the blank; for sentence insertion, find the clue (pronoun, time marker, connector) | Check the choice against the whole text |
| 7 | Read the question first, then find the line that holds the answer | Match your answer to the text, not to memory |

Timing and habits (indicative, not a promise):
- Reading is one continuous 75-minute block with no section break, so students choose how to spend the time. A common plan is to finish Parts 5 and 6 in roughly 20 to 25 minutes and keep the rest for Part 7.
- Students can skip a Part 7 set and come back.
- Students answer every question; there is no penalty for a wrong answer.
- Check these figures against ETS's examinee handbook before they are published to students.

## Skills: the English behind the strategy
| Part | Skill areas | Linked types |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Word families and suffix patterns tied to position; verb forms and agreement; prepositions, confused pairs and fixed phrases | TQ-5-1 to TQ-5-3 |
| 6 | Tenses and agreement across a text; linking words and pronoun reference; email and notice conventions | TQ-6-1 to TQ-6-3 |
| 7 | Skimming and scanning; paraphrase recognition; document types (email, notice, advertisement, schedule, text-message chain) | TQ-7-1 to TQ-7-3 |

### Skills for every Reading part
| Group | Skill areas |
|---|---|
| Chinese-speaker challenges (explained with zh-TW notes) | Articles and plurals; tense and agreement; word class |
| Vocabulary growth | Workplace word lists by domain; word-family review; spaced repetition |
| Test stamina | Reading speed; pacing across 75 minutes; staying focused through Part 7 |

Skills carry more weight here than in IELTS, especially Parts 5 and 6, because those parts test the language directly.
