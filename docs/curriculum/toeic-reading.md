# TOEIC Reading (Parts 5 to 7)

Two sections, kept separate and cross-linked. **Strategy** is how students work through the test. **Skills** is the English that makes the strategy work.

## Strategy: the two pillars
Same architecture as IELTS: **Locate**, then **Answer**.

| Pillar | Part 5 | Part 6 | Part 7 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Locate | Read the words around the blank and the options | Read the surrounding sentences for the clue | Read the question first, then find the line that holds the answer |
| Answer | Choose by form first, then meaning | Check the choice against the whole text | Match your answer to the text, not to memory |

Timing guide (indicative, not a promise): move quickly through Part 5 and Part 6 so there is time left for Part 7, where the passages are longest. Students answer every question; there is no penalty for a wrong answer.

## Skills: the English behind the strategy
| Part | Skill areas | Linked types |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Word families and forms; prepositions and conjunctions; workplace vocabulary and collocations | TQ-5-1 to TQ-5-3 |
| 6 | Tenses and agreement across a text; linking words; email and notice conventions | TQ-6-1 to TQ-6-3 |
| 7 | Business document types (email, notice, advertisement, schedule, chat); reading speed; paraphrase | TQ-7-1 to TQ-7-3 |

Skills here carry more weight than in IELTS, especially Parts 5 and 6, because those parts test the language directly.

## Phase 4 build order
Part 5, then Part 6, then Part 7 (see `docs/TOEIC-PLAN.md`).
