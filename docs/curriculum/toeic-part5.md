## Strategy
**Spot the clue**
1. Read the three or four words on each side of the blank before you look at the options.
2. Decide what the blank needs: a noun, a verb, an adjective, an adverb, or a small function word.
3. Glance at the options: forms of one word mean a form question; different words mean a meaning question.

**Choose and move on**
1. Form questions: match the word class the position needs, then check agreement and tense.
2. Meaning questions: choose the word that works with its neighbours, then read the whole sentence once.
3. About twenty seconds per question. Mark a guess, note the number, come back only if time is left.

Keep in mind: form before meaning; the right option makes a natural sentence; every question gets an answer.

## Skills
| Group | Three things to teach |
|---|---|
| Word forms | Suffix patterns tied to position; verb forms and agreement; participles as adjectives |
| Function words | Prepositions of time and place; connectors (despite, although, however, therefore); pairs and relatives (neither/nor, who/which) |
| Workplace vocabulary | Collocations; fixed patterns (have access to, notify someone of, respond to, compatible with); confusable words |
